# Pricing

Knijn Space has two tiers. Both run on the same infrastructure: Amsterdam (ams1) and Frankfurt (ffm1), with DNS on Cloudflare.

## Free

- Best-effort hosting, shared resources.
- No uptime or support-response guarantees, but service stays up as long as I'm able to keep it up. Status is always visible at [status.knijn.one](https://status.knijn.one).
- Free migration help from your existing host, regardless of tier.
- Subject to the [Terms of Service](/tos/). Don't abuse it and we're good.

**[Email emma@knijn.one to get started](mailto:emma@knijn.one)**

## Paid

Everything in Free, plus:

- Dedicated resources (your own allocated CPU, RAM, and storage)
- Guaranteed support response times
- A monthly uptime target with service credits if missed

Full specifics: [Paid Plan Guarantees](/paid-guarantees/).

Pricing depends on the resources you need. Email [emma@knijn.one](mailto:emma@knijn.one) with what you're looking to host and I'll put together a quote.

## Need a node somewhere else?

Current infrastructure is in Amsterdam and Frankfurt. If your project needs a different region for latency or jurisdiction reasons, reach out. I'm open to standing up a new node for the right use case, and it won't cost you anything extra if the project is on the free tier.
