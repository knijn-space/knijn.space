# Paid Plan Guarantees

These are the specifics of what the paid tier includes, on top of everything in the [Free tier](/pricing/). This page is informational and describes current service commitments. It isn't part of the [Terms of Service](/tos/), so it may be adjusted from time to time as the paid offering evolves; the ToS itself governs the legal relationship between us.

## Dedicated resources

Your CPU, RAM, and storage allocation is dedicated to your project and not shared with other users on the same node.

## Support response

Support requests from paid customers are acknowledged within 3 business days.

## Uptime target

99% monthly uptime, excluding scheduled maintenance, measured against [status.knijn.one](https://status.knijn.one). If uptime falls below this in a given month, affected customers can request a service credit toward their next billing period.

## Migration help

Free for both tiers. If you're moving from another host, I'll help you get set up at no extra cost.
