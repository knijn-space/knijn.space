## Infrastructure

I run VPS nodes in **Amsterdam, Netherlands (ams1)** and **Frankfurt, Germany (ffm1)**, with DNS managed through Cloudflare, plus Cloudflare Pages for static deployments. If you have a specific latency or jurisdiction need, get in touch. I'm open to spinning up a node elsewhere on request for the right project.

## FAQ

**Q:** Is this free?

**A:** Free hosting is still available, as long as you ask nicely and don't abuse resources. If you need dedicated resources or guaranteed support response times, check out the [paid tier](/pricing/).

**Q:** What's the catch?

**A:** None. Free-tier sites stay up as long as I'm physically able to keep them up. You'll need to follow the [ToS](/tos/).

**Q:** How do I sign up?

**A:** Email me at [emma@knijn.one](mailto:emma@knijn.one), or reach out through any of the contact methods on [knijn.one](https://knijn.one), and we'll figure out what works.

**Q:** Do you help migrate existing sites?

**A:** Yes, free of charge, regardless of tier.

**Q:** What technologies do you use?

**A:** Cloudflare Pages for static deployments (like this site), and VPS nodes in Amsterdam and Frankfurt for everything else. DNS is managed through Cloudflare, though not every service is proxied through it.
