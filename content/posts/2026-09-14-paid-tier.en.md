# Introducing Paid Hosting on Knijn Space

Free hosting on Knijn Space isn't going anywhere, it's still here, still free, and still works the way it always has. Alongside it, I'm now offering a paid tier for people who need a bit more.

## What's new

The paid tier adds:

- **Dedicated resources.** Your own allocated CPU, RAM, and storage, not shared with other users on the same node.
- **Support guarantee.** Support requests acknowledged within 3 business days.
- **Uptime target.** 99% monthly uptime, excluding scheduled maintenance, tracked against [status.knijn.one](https://status.knijn.one). If a month falls short, affected customers can request a service credit toward their next bill.
- **Free migration help**, same as the free tier already offers. Moving from another host doesn't cost extra either way.

Full details are on the new [Pricing](/pricing/) page, with the specifics of what's guaranteed on [Paid Plan Guarantees](/paid-guarantees/).

## Infrastructure

Both tiers run on the same infrastructure: VPS nodes in **Amsterdam, Netherlands** and **Frankfurt, Germany**. If your project needs a different region, get in touch. I'm open to standing up a node elsewhere for the right use case, and that's free if the project itself is on the free tier.

## Pricing

There's no fixed price list yet. Email [emma@knijn.one](mailto:emma@knijn.one) with what you're looking to host and I'll put together a quote based on the resources you need.
