Knijn Space now has a [privacy notice](/privacy/). It covers what personal data I handle when you host with me or visit this site, why I need it, who else processes it, how long I keep it, and how to exercise your rights.
<!--more-->

Two practical things came with it:

- **Data processing agreement.** If your hosted site collects personal data from its own visitors, I can sign a data processing agreement. Just ask. Paid plans get one with the quote.
- **Security contact.** There's now a [security.txt](/.well-known/security.txt), so if you find a security problem, you know where to send it.

The Terms of Service haven't changed. Questions about any of this: [emma@knijn.one](mailto:emma@knijn.one).
