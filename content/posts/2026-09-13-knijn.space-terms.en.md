# Terms of Service Update - 2026-09-27

We're updating our [Terms of Service](/tos), effective 2026-09-27. This post summarizes what's changing and why. If you don't agree with the updated terms, you can terminate your hosting before they take effect without penalty. See the Amendments section below for how that works going forward.

## What's changing

**Acceptable use**
- The prohibited activities list is more specific now: we've added cryptomining/excessive resource abuse, malware/phishing hosting, circumventing security or rate limits, privacy-violating or harassing content, unauthorized reselling of our services, and account credential sharing. These were mostly implied before; we're naming them explicitly.
- Commercial/for-profit use is now clarified: it's meant for individuals and small groups (clubs, hobby projects), not registered companies or larger organisations, unless we agree to it in writing beforehand.

**Age requirement**
- The minimum age to use our services is now 16, up from 13, in line with the age of consent for data processing under Dutch/EU law (GDPR/UAVG). Users under 16 need a parent or guardian's consent.

**Support and availability**
- We're introducing a support commitment for paying customers: support requests will be acknowledged within 3 business days. This isn't an uptime guarantee (service is still provided as-is) but it's a concrete response-time commitment we didn't have before.

**Data handling**
- We've clarified that after your hosting is terminated, your data is deleted within 7 days (unless we're legally required to keep it longer).
- Backups remain your responsibility. We don't guarantee backup availability.
- We've added a note on the contact info we keep (your email, and optionally a secondary contact like Discord) and why: to reach you about billing, support, and changes like this one. Email is the authoritative channel for official notices.
- We've noted that some of our infrastructure providers may process data outside the EEA, with appropriate safeguards in place where that happens.

**Termination**
- We've committed to making reasonable efforts to notify you before suspending or terminating your hosting, except in cases of severe or ongoing violations, where we may need to act immediately.

**Future changes to these terms**
- Going forward, material changes to the Terms of Service will come with at least 14 days' notice by email, and you'll have the right to terminate before the changes take effect if you don't agree with them. Minor changes (typo fixes, clarifications) may go live without advance notice.

## Why now

Most of these changes exist to make the terms clearer and fairer as we move toward offering paid hosting, things like the support commitment and the guaranteed right to terminate before future changes take effect are new protections for you, not just us covering ourselves.
