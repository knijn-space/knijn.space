## How pricing works

You pay upfront for a number of months, in cash, in person. The total is the monthly price times the number of months. Knijn Space is a hobby project run by me, not a registered business, so I don't charge VAT.

Pick an example below. The terms that apply to every quote are at the bottom.

## Terms for every quote

**Payment.** Plans are prepaid for the number of months in the quote, in cash, in person. When you accept, we agree a time and place. I give you a written receipt with the date, the amount and the period it covers, and service starts, at the latest, once I've received payment.

**Commitments.** Every quote includes the [Paid Plan Service Commitments](/paid-guarantees/). They apply for the period you've paid for, even if the Terms of Service or the commitments page change later. If a quote and the [Terms of Service](/tos/) conflict about these commitments, the quote wins. Everything else stays under the ToS.

**Renewal and stopping.** Plans don't renew automatically. I'll email you at least 14 days before your paid period ends, and the price in your quote doesn't change during the period you've paid for. Unused prepaid months aren't refunded, except in the cases listed in the commitments page.

**Accepting a quote.** Reply to the email the quote came with, from the address I have on file, saying "Accepted" and the quote number. Then we arrange the payment.
